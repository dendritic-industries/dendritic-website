Pushing the frontiers of manufacturing

Massive Metal Manufacturing

We are team of engineers building the next generation of large-scale hybrid manufacturing, with a simple goal: make a 10-ton component by just uploading a file and clicking "Run". The road to achieve this will not be simple, but even today we can fundamentally change how parts are made at this scale.

Unleashing Heavy Industry

While CNC machining and powder-based additive have revolutionized manufacturing at the smaller scale, the largest and most critical parts for heavy industry are still made via the same casting and forging techniques employed over a century ago. Lead times are long, properties vary, design freedom is limited.

We can change that, slashing lead times from months to days, enabling direct control of properties, and freeing engineers to use optimal geometries.

From the Ground Up

Our manufacturing stack is designed in-house and vertically integrated, from motion control to metallurgical planning. We believe this gives us the maximum freedom to build what is needed, instead of just building around what has come before.

Rooted in our fundamental metallurgical, process control, and optimization research, we bring the benefits of the bleeding edge to our customers.
